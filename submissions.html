Open saved video ↗

Share the video file itself. In Google Drive, set General access to Anyone with the link and choose Viewer. Check the link in a private browser window before submitting.

How to share your video

  1. Upload your video file to Google Drive, then open the file’s Share menu.
  2. Under General access, select Anyone with the link and set the role to Viewer.
  3. Choose Copy link and Done. Open the copied link in a private/incognito browser window and confirm that the video plays without signing in or requesting access.
  4. Log in here, open the relevant competition and paste that video file link into the submission field. Select Save submission.

Share the individual video file link, rather than a folder link. Keep the file and its viewing permission available until judging is complete.

Before you submit
