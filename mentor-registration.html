Preparing the student workbook

Use the Students sheet: enter state and city once at the top, then one student per row. The Instructions sheet explains the participant groups and dropdowns.

  • Student email addresses may repeat within the same school. Each student needs a different phone number.
  • Use 10-digit Indian mobile numbers. This template is for Bhagavad Gita Quiz only.
  • Open the .xlsx file in a spreadsheet app and enable editing. Select a state, gender or participant-group cell for its dropdown.
  • Upload .xlsx files up to 2 MB. Uploading collects the workbook; student registrations will be processed later.
