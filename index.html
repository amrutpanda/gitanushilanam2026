Gitanushilanam09
Competition Detail
Bhagavad Gita Quiz

A multi-round quiz designed to combine knowledge, speed and reflection.

Round 1

Written Screening

  • 30 minutes · 30 marks
  • MCQ + subjective
  • Offline: paper based
  • Online: timed Google Form / screening platform
  • Offline top 3–5 per age group advance
Round 2

Kahoot Quiz

  • Completely online
  • Individual participation
  • Qualifiers from Round 1
  • Judged by maximum marks scored
Round 3

3-Minute Video

  • Shortlisted candidates upload a video
  • Topic to be finalized
  • Round 2 + Round 3 marks combine to decide winners

Syllabus & Format

Syllabus: Bhagavad Gita As It Is — Chapters 1 and 2.
Study material: age-group slides and material based on Sir’s lectures and Bhagavad Gita As It Is.
Reference: Bhagavad-Gita As It Is; Chapter 2 of Five Aspects of Absolute Truth.
Medium: English.
Quiz age bands: Grades 3–5, 6–8, 9–12, Young Adult and Senior Adult.
