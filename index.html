The spirit of Gitanushilanam

Wisdom becomes meaningful when it is explored, remembered and lived.

Gitanushilanam brings the Bhagavad Gita into an engaging learning experience through quiz, shloka recitation and a discovery-based treasure hunt. The programme is rooted in the Bhagavad Gita As It Is and the Learn Gita Live Gita learning ecosystem.

Three flagship experiences

Choose your way to engage with the Gita.

Competition structures below are based on the latest publicly available Gitanushilanam format. The 2026 schedule and final syllabus will be updated when officially announced.

01 · Knowledge

Bhagavad Gita Quiz

Test understanding of key teachings, concepts and deeper themes through a two-stage competitive format.

  • Basic: ideal for beginners
  • Advanced: for deeper study
  • Screening round + Final round
02 · Recitation

Shloka Recitation

Develop clarity, confidence and devotion through structured Sanskrit recitation with progressive levels.

  • Beginner: open-book format
  • Intermediate: memorized recitation
  • Advanced: recitation + translation
03 · Discovery

Rahasyam Treasure Hunt

Solve clues, research ideas and uncover hidden knowledge in a digital riddle hunt inspired by Indian wisdom traditions.

  • For school & college participants
  • English and Hindi questions
  • Research + problem-solving focus

Latest published format

Bhagavad Gita Quiz

The public 2025 guidelines describe two open participation levels. The 2026 final syllabus and dates are pending official release.

Basic Level

For beginners and participants new to systematic Gita study. The previous published syllabus used Bhagavad Gita Chapters 1–2 and Learn Gita Live Gita Chapters 1 and 4.

Advanced Level

For regular readers and deeper study. The previous published syllabus used Bhagavad Gita Chapters 3–6 and Learn Gita Live Gita Chapters 4–6.

Published format: Screening round followed by a Final round for qualifying participants.

Latest published format

Bhagavad Gita Shloka Recitation

Beginner

Open-book shloka recitation with translation.

Intermediate

Memorized shloka recitation, with the previous rules requiring eyes closed in the submitted video.

Advanced

Memorized shloka recitation with translation, also following continuous-video rules.

Previous evaluation: fluency, pronunciation and presentation. The 2026 prescribed chapter and submission rules should be confirmed before publishing.

Latest published format

Rahasyam · Bhagavad Gita Treasure Hunt

The latest public page presents a digital riddle-solving event for tech-savvy school and college students, with questions available in English and Hindi.

  • Research and book/online reference were permitted in the published rules.
  • Questions draw on Indian culture, history, Ramayana and Mahabharata themes.
  • Participants solve clues and submit the correct keyword or answer.

The 2026 round structure, dates and eligibility should be updated after official confirmation.
