The spirit of Gitanushilanam

Study deeply. Recite beautifully. Create thoughtfully. Discover joyfully.

Gitanushilanam turns Bhagavad Gita learning into an engaging participant journey for every participants. The programme combines guided learning with competitive rounds, age-appropriate pathways and a grand closing celebration with quiz and recitation syllabus directly from Bhagavad Gita As it is.

Four competition streams

Paths to connecting with the wisdom of Gita.

The 2026 programme offers four ways to engage with the Bhagavad Gita: Quiz, Shloka Recitation, Gita Reel Making Challenge and Treasure Hunt. Each stream combines learning, creativity, participation and discovery.

01 · Knowledge

Bhagavad Gita Quiz

Test your understanding of Bhagavad Gita As It Is, Chapters 1 and 2, through three individual rounds.

  • 10-minute MCQ screening: 5–6 December
  • Kahoot round: 12–13 December
  • Zoom final: 3 January 2027
Explore 2026 rules ↗
02 · Recitation

Shloka Recitation

Two focused rounds that move from a submitted recitation to a live Zoom performance for selected candidates.

  • Video submission by 6 December
  • Selected participants proceed to live Zoom
  • Group-specific shlokas and translations
Explore 2026 rules ↗
BG 03 · Creativity

Gita Reel Making Challenge

Bring Krishna’s Virat Roopa to life in an original animated Reel, using any technology.

  • Topic: Krishna's Virat Roopa (BG 11.9 - 46)
  • Maximum duration: 90 seconds
  • Vertical format: 9:16
  • Submit by 10 December 2026
View submission details ↗
04 · Discovery

Treasure Hunt

Follow clues inspired by the Bhagavad Gita, Ramayana and Mahabharata in an individual journey of learning and discovery.

  • Open to Grades 9–12 and adults
  • Individual participation
  • Event date to be announced

Simple, guided participation

From registration to recognition.

The programme is designed as a guided sequence: register, prepare through online classes, participate in the relevant rounds, and celebrate the winners together.

Preparation matters Age-group learning modules and lectures will help you prepare. Class dates will be confirmed; use each competition guide for its syllabus and round dates.
  1. 01

    Register

    Complete the primary registration phase by 31 October 2026.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Study the prescribed Gita material and join age-group online learning modules.

  3. 03

    Compete

    Take part in the applicable quiz, recitation, creative-video or treasure-hunt rounds.

  4. 04

    Advance

    Qualifiers progress through competition-specific online and submission stages.

  5. 05

    Celebrate

    Join the final online ceremony, with the programme culminating in the tentative Vrindavan trip.

Our inspiration

Carrying forward a tradition of learning and devotion.

Founder-Acharya of ISKCON

His Divine Grace A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada

Widely respected as a revolutionary Vedic scholar, translator and teacher, and as the Founder-Acharya of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON). His work brought the bhakti-yoga tradition and the teachings of Krishna, as presented in India’s ancient Vedic writings, to readers and students around the world.

Senior disciple of Srila Prabhupada

His Holiness Gopal Krishna Goswami Maharaj

A key inspiration behind Bhagavad Gita learning initiatives and the sharing of Gita wisdom with students and communities.

Dr. Laxmidhar Behera (Dr. Lila Purushottam Das)

Director, IIT Mandi · Director, Bhagavad Gita Pathshala

Dr. Laxmidhar Behera, also known as Dr. Lila Purushottam Das, previously served as a senior professor at IIT Kanpur and is a researcher in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics. He has spent more than 25 years working in education and sharing Vedic and spiritual wisdom with audiences in India and abroad.

He holds a PhD from IIT Delhi and has published more than 120 research papers. Alongside his academic work, he has been deeply involved in initiatives that connect education, character development and the teachings of the Bhagavad Gita.
