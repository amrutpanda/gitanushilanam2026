Who can participate?

The Treasure Hunt is open to everyone aged 10 years and above. There is no upper age limit.

Participation is individual. You may take help from the internet, books and family members while solving the riddles.

Follow the clues

During the event you will receive a series of task-based riddles. Each riddle comes as text, an image or a video, and points to a character, event, teaching or place from the scriptures.

Work out the answer, complete the task the riddle asks for, and capture it as a photo or video. Careful reading, observation and reasoning will take you further than speed alone.

How each puzzle works

Every item in the hunt is a puzzle followed by a task. A puzzle may be a riddle, cipher, word jumble, fill-in-the-blank, or emoji or picture puzzle. Solve it to find out what to do, perform that task, and upload your photo or video.

Types of tasks

  • Arrange items: one clear photo of items from home laid out as the riddle asks.
  • Recite verses: a video of you chanting the verse clearly, followed by its meaning in one line.
  • Act a scene: a 30 to 60 second video; family members may play the other roles.
  • Interview people: a video of about 1 minute where you ask a family member or elder a question, then sum up their reply.
  • Find it on Google Maps: a screenshot of the place or route the puzzle points to. Maps screenshots count as photo answers.
  • Write and show: a photo of you holding a paper with the answer written on it.

These are only examples; puzzles can lead to other kinds of tasks too. Tasks are checked by hand, partial marks are possible, and harder tasks carry more points. Say the riddle number at the start of each video, ask permission before recording anyone, and avoid fire, sharp tools or climbing.

What you'll need: a phone with a camera and Google Maps, paper and a pen, and common household items. No drawing or craft skill is needed. Every task can be done alone; family help is optional.

What to study

  • Bhagavad Gita As It Is by A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada
  • Ramayana
  • Mahabharata
  • Srimad Bhagavatam

Puzzles may also cover the temples, holy places, festivals, art and history connected to these scriptures.

Participants are encouraged to study Bhagavad Gita As It Is and Srimad Bhagavatam by His Divine Grace A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada.

Event information

Event date: Sunday, 6 December 2026, 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM IST.

At 4:00 PM, riddles will be shared through the participants' WhatsApp groups, along with a Google Form for submitting answers. There are 10 to 20 riddles, all task-based, and you can solve them in any order.

Submit all answers before 6:00 PM IST sharp; late entries are not scored. We check the YouTube upload time of every video, so videos uploaded or added to your playlist after the deadline will not count.

How to submit

Every answer is a photo or a video. Some tasks also ask for a short typed answer, such as a name, distance or verse number, so the Google Form has a text box for each riddle as well as the upload.

Photos

Upload on the Google Form shared in your WhatsApp group. Name each file with the riddle number and your name, for example Riddle03_Radha.jpg.

Videos

  1. Record one short video per riddle. Begin by saying your name and the riddle number.
  2. Upload each video to YouTube as Unlisted (not Private, or the judges cannot view it).
  3. Title it: TH2026 - Riddle <number> - <Your name>.
  4. Put all your videos, in riddle order, into one Unlisted playlist named Treasure Hunt 2026 - <Your name>.
  5. Submit the playlist link on the same Google Form.

Tip: open your playlist in a private browser window before submitting. If you can watch every video without signing in, so can the judges.

Judging and rules

Riddle round: 80%. Scored on correct answers and how well each task is completed.

Interview: 20%. Selected top scorers will be interviewed by phone call or Zoom within 1 to 2 days of the competition (7 to 8 December), about the scriptures behind their answers. The number of candidates is decided after the riddle round. The winner is decided on the combined score.

  • Photos and videos must be recorded by you or your family during the event.
  • Do not share answers with other participants.
  • Keep all content respectful and suitable for all ages.
  • One entry per participant; if you submit twice, the last entry before the deadline counts.
  • An answer the judges cannot open scores zero.
  • The organisers' decision is final.
