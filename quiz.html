Who can participate?

Open to Subjunior (Grades 3–5), Junior (Grades 6–8), Senior (Grades 9–12) and Youth / Adult (all adult participants).

Individual participation. The quiz medium is English.

Your three rounds

Round 01

Online MCQ screening

5 December 2026, 12 noon → 6 December 2026, 12 noon. The access window lasts 24 hours; your quiz attempt lasts 10 minutes. The timezone will be confirmed before exam access opens.

Answer and submit the MCQs through the online exam link within the allotted time. Participants with the highest scores qualify for Round 2.

Exam access coming soon. The exam link and access instructions will be published here.

Round 02

Live Kahoot quiz

  • 12 December 2026: Grades 3–5 and Grades 6–8.
  • 13 December 2026: Grades 9–12 and all adult participants.

Exact timings will be communicated to selected participants after Round 1. Maximum marks scored determine qualification for the final.

Final round

Personal Zoom interview

3 January 2027. Qualifiers from Round 2 appear individually for an online interview via Zoom. Judging is based on maximum marks scored.

Scoring and progression

Maximum marks scored are the judging criterion for each round. Top scorers advance from the first round; qualifiers from the second round enter the final. The number of qualifying participants has not been announced.
