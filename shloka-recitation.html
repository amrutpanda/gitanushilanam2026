From recording to live recitation

Round 01

Record your recitation

Submission deadline: 6 December 2026. Prepare the syllabus for your participant group and record your shloka recitation.

Upload your video to your own Drive account. You will share its link through the website submission page when it opens.

Submission access coming soon. Link-sharing instructions and the submission form will be published here. Registration does not submit your video.

Round 02

Live screening on Zoom

Selected candidates from Round 1 will be invited to a live screening on Zoom. The event date and Zoom link will be communicated individually.

Recording guidance

Detailed recording instructions, including verse coverage, duration, editing rules and judging criteria, will be published before submissions open. No fixed number of shortlisted candidates has been announced.
